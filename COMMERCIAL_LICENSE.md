## Why buy one

PagedMoE’s public grant is [AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE). That is intentionally
strong: it keeps the project inspectable and contribution-friendly while
blocking silent closed-source commercialization of the engine.

If your company needs to use PagedMoE (or a derivative) in a
**proprietary product**, **closed binary**, or **hosted service** without
AGPL source-disclosure obligations, you need a **commercial license**.

## What you typically get

Exact terms are negotiated, but a commercial grant usually includes:

- Non-exclusive right to use, modify, and distribute PagedMoE in
  proprietary products / services without AGPL copyleft obligations
- Permission to keep your modifications private (as specified in the
  signed agreement)
- Clear copyright ownership remaining with Noah Clark; you receive a
  license, not an assignment
- Optional: support, priority fixes, or feature work (scoped separately)

What you do **not** get by default: exclusive rights, ownership of the
upstream project, or the right to relicense the public AGPL edition.

## What stays free

- Evaluating and researching the AGPL edition
- Personal / open-source use under AGPL-3.0
- Academic work that complies with AGPL-3.0
- Publishing AGPL-compatible improvements back to the community
